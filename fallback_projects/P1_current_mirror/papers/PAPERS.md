# Papers for Project 1 (low-voltage high-accuracy current mirror)

The cloud machine could not download PDFs (publisher sites are blocked from it), so this file gives the
citation, DOI and open-access link of each paper. Every link marked **OA** is free to download.

## Base paper
- **J. Ramírez-Angulo, A. Paul, M. Gangineni, J. M. Hinojo-Montero, J. Huerta-Chua, "Class AB Voltage Follower and
  Low-Voltage Current Mirror with Very High Figures of Merit Based on the Flipped Voltage Follower,"**
  *J. Low Power Electron. Appl.* 13(2):28, 24 Apr 2023. doi:10.3390/jlpea13020028.
  **OA:** https://www.mdpi.com/2079-9268/13/2/28 (PDF button on that page).
  180 nm CMOS, 1 V, post-layout. Mirror: FVF cascode + two auxiliary folded-cascode amplifiers + FVF level shifter.
  Reported: Rin 0.212 Ω, Rout 112 GΩ, gain accuracy better than 0.01 %, BW 144 MHz, input 0.15 V, output compliance 0.2 V,
  noise 14.5 pA/√Hz, Ibias 2 µA, mirror devices 5/0.4 µm. Future work they name: a class AB version, double-cascoded auxiliaries.
